7Strategy — the plan in 6 lines

  • PYQs are king. Solve all 12 papers twice; ISRO recycles questions heavily. Keep an error log and re-test weak spots.
  • Follow weightage. Top 5 subjects ≈ 51 of ~80 marks — finish those to a high level first.
  • 1 min/question. Practice timed. With −1/3 marking, guess only after eliminating 2+ options.
  • Don't skip applied topics (Cybersecurity, AI/ML, Big Data, Cloud, Web, Graphics) — ~18+ marks beyond standard GATE CSE.
  • Part B is free marks — 15 min/day of aptitude keeps it sharp; usually no negative marking.
  • Last 2 weeks = revision + mocks only. Re-attempt GfG quizzes timed; review the error log daily.
